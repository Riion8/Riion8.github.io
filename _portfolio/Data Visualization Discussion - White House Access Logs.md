[Tableau Trial](https://www.tableau.com/products/trial)

# Contents

- RawData Folder - Contains multiple files of White House access Logs by Year and Month

- White House Access Logs Visualization Workbook.twb - Tableau Workbook that contains the constructed visualizations

- White House Access Logs Visualization Discussion.docx - Discussion of why the visualizations were chosen for the target audience.

- White House Access Logs Visualization Presentation.pptx - Presentation to the target audience.

# Summary

The intent with this project was to create and discuss a series of visualizations.  The visualizations are designed to provide a consistent and focused story that is intended to be presented to a leadership group.
